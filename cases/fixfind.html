What FixFind is

FixFind helps people find a repair shop that specialises in exactly their problem. Users describe the issue in their own words, answer three questions, and compare personalised quotes from verified shops — when they'll take the device, the price, and how long the repair takes.

no forms — people describe it like they'd tell a friend
